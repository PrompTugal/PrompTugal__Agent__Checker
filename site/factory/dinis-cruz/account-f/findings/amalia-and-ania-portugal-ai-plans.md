# amalia-and-ania-portugal-ai-plans: the S88 update note (28 November): one small finding

Checked 2026-11-28T08:14Z by cl-6.checker@PrompTugal.dinis against ECO's page (fetched that day; the text is in the HTML, not behind script). For: reviewer@PrompTugal.vicky (published it, 08:05 UTC) and the article's author desk. Only the update note was checked, not the whole article.

1. **"These are different sources' figures and we do not reconcile them."** The figures do reconcile, and ECO says so: "O novo financiamento eleva o custo total do Amália para 7,3 milhões de euros" (the new funding raises AMALIA's total cost to 7.3 million euros). The article's 5.5 million euros (INESC-ID and NOVA FCT, for the first phase) plus the added 1.8 million euros is 7.3 million. The sources do not state "5.5 + 1.8 = 7.3", so saying it is our arithmetic, but "we do not reconcile them" reads as if the sources disagree when ECO's own sentence implies the earlier cost was 5.5. Fix: "5.5 million euros (first phase, as INESC-ID and NOVA FCT gave it) plus the 1.8 million euros added makes the 7.3 million euros ECO gives; ECO does not give the earlier figure itself."

Checks out: ECO's report of Tuesday 15 September 2026 (the URL date; "esta terça-feira" and the resolution "publicada esta terça-feira no Diário da República"); the second phase made official; 1.8 million euros from the Recovery and Resilience Plan (PRR), backdated to 1 July; total 7.3 million euros; the government's aims by the end of 2026 ("ferramentas de agentificação", "soluções passíveis de serem integradas em processos internos da Administração Pública", image and speech use cases); the government's main aim quoted "o Amália deixe de ser apenas um protótipo e passe a estar integrado no atendimento dos cidadãos"; the 9 September government approval; the planned talks with media companies on their latest archives; the Journalists' Union contesting access to Arquivo.pt in the first phase. Not checked: the Council of Ministers resolution itself (the note says it was not read either); that the article gave 5.5 million euros "from INESC-ID and NOVA FCT" I did verify (lines 55, 78 and 79 of the article).
