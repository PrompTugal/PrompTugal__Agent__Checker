## 1. INOVA Algarve 3.0: 15 called, 15 selected plus 5 extra, 20 in all: confirmed

- **The page** says the free consulting call is for "up to 15" tourism SMEs ("a support service, not a direct financial grant or State aid", "Zero Cost"), and has two sections: "SELECTED 15 ALGARVIAN COMPANIES, PHASE 1" and "SELECTED +5 EXTRA ALGARVIAN COMPANIES, PHASE 2", each with a PDF.
- **The PDFs (opened and counted):** "Candidaturas selecionadas I" lists 15 entries; "Candidaturas SelecionadasII" lists 5. So 20 are listed as selected, as the researcher found. The entries are numbered 1 to 22 across the two lists, with numbers 6 and 8 absent; the page does not say why (we do not know whether those were not selected, withdrew, or were left out). Both PDFs are headed "Projeto INOVA Algarve 3.0, Programa piloto, Plano de ação para a IA e robótica".
- **What the lists contain:** each entry gives a legal name, a trading name, a tax number and a municipality. Several are sole traders named as individuals. Do not publish those names or the tax numbers; counts, trading names of companies and municipalities are enough, and the page itself is the source to link.
- **Researcher's note, two corrections:** (a) it says "who leads the pilot is not stated on the page"; the page says the Digital Transition Program for Algarve tourism companies is "an activity led by Algarve Evolution within the scope of this project". (b) It does not say when the call opened, how many applied, or when the selections were made; the page does not give them.
- **Consortium, checked:** NERA, Algarve Evolution, Universidade do Algarve, Região de Turismo do Algarve, CCDR Algarve, KIPT and Tertúlia Algarvia, under the SIAC Incentive System (Support System for Collective Actions): as the researcher wrote.
- **One thing to weigh, not confirmed:** the second list includes a company whose trading name is "Tertúlia Algarvia", the same name as a consortium member. They may be the same organisation; the page does not say. If they are, a consortium member is among the selected, which a story should state and the organisation should be asked about. Not for us to assert.
- **The studies:** the page describes them as "real-world global case studies, simulated for our region". The PDFs (INOVA Estudo, Resumido) were not read.
